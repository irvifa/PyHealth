# ProtoECGNet rhythm branch: combined Mac + Kaggle results (6 seeds)

Same setup as `../rhythm_mac/mac_results.md` (rhythm branch, 16 labels, 5
global prototypes per label, 20 / 20 / 15 epochs, lr 1e-3, batch 32, folds
1–8 / 9 / 10). Seeds 0–2 ran on a MacBook Pro M4 Pro (MPS), seeds 3–5 on a
Kaggle Tesla T4 (CUDA). The configs differ only in paths, seeds and device.
`summary.md` is generated by `branch_seed_sweep.py --summarize-only`.

| Black-box | 0.9368 ± 0.0034 | 0.9178 ± 0.0032 | 0.9403 |
| No contrastive | 0.9308 ± 0.0105 | 0.9105 ± 0.0025 | 0.8903 |

## H2: contrastive gain under an equal budget

| Seed | Device | Macro gain | Bootstrap 95% CI | Weighted gain | Macro gain, labels with ≥10 positives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | MPS | −0.0053 | [−0.0143, +0.0043] | −0.0197 | −0.0090 |
| 1 | MPS | −0.0004 | [−0.0080, +0.0068] | −0.0098 | +0.0022 |
| 2 | MPS | −0.0109 | [−0.0191, −0.0030] | −0.0155 | −0.0201 |
| 3 | T4 | +0.0237 | [−0.0082, +0.0548] | −0.0090 | −0.0006 |
| 4 | T4 | −0.0042 | [−0.0138, +0.0027] | −0.0038 | −0.0055 |
| 5 | T4 | −0.0186 | [−0.0412, +0.0024] | −0.0033 | −0.0002 |
| **Mean ± sd** | | **−0.0026 ± 0.0131** | | **−0.0102** | **−0.0056 ± 0.0082** |

The paper reports +0.0161 from one run with unequal tuning.

**Seed 3 is a rare-label artefact.** Its no-contrastive model scored 0.574
AUROC on TRIGU, a label with only 2 test positives; the other no-contrastive
runs scored 0.92–0.99. That one label moves macro AUROC by about 0.025, which
is the whole of seed 3's +0.024. Seed 5's −0.019 is mostly the mirror image
(contrastive TRIGU 0.677). Restricting to the 9 labels with at least 10 test
positives (1AVB, AFIB, IVCD, PACE, SARRH, SBRAD, SR, STACH, SVARR) removes
this noise.

**Conclusion: H2 is not supported.** Across 6 equal-budget seeds the
contrastive loss gives no macro-AUROC gain (median −0.0047; negative in 5 of
6 seeds, and in 4 of 6 on well-populated labels, with the other two ≈ 0),
and lowers weighted AUROC in 6 of 6 seeds (mean −0.010).

## H3-style margin (branch level)

Black-box − contrastive = +0.0087 ± 0.0056, inside the proposal's 0.015
margin. The paper's gap was +0.0339.

## Comparison with the paper

- Black-box: 0.9368 vs 0.9403 (−0.0035, about one seed std).
- Both prototype variants beat the paper's numbers (+0.041 and +0.022). The
  15-epoch classifier stage is one plausible cause; it was not tested.
- Mac seeds score slightly higher than Kaggle seeds for the black-box
  (0.9394 vs 0.9343). That is within normal device/seed variation and does not
  change any conclusion.
